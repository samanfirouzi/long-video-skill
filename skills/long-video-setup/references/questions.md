# Q&A question bank

Ask in groups, in this order. Each question has a default. Skip questions made irrelevant by earlier answers.

## Group 1: Video basics

1. **Format.** Options: explainer, documentary, storytelling, tutorial, other. Default: storytelling.
2. **Target length.** Options: 5-10 min, 10-20 min, 20-40 min, 40+ min. Default: 10-20 min.
3. **Language.** Default: English.
4. **Platform.** Options: YouTube, other. Default: YouTube.
5. **Aspect ratio, resolution and frame rate.** Default: 16:9, 1080p, 24 fps.
6. **Clip length.** Longest single shot, and the preferred range. Default: max 10 seconds, preferred 5 to 8 seconds. Explain that video generators make short clips, so this limit shapes the script.

## Group 2: Audience and tone

1. **Audience.** Free text. Examples: curious beginners, professionals, kids. Default: general adults.
2. **Tone.** Options: serious, friendly, humorous, dramatic, calm. Default: friendly.
3. **Narration point of view.** Options: narrator, first person, character voices. Default: narrator.

## Group 3: Visual style

1. **Visual approach.** Options: stock footage, AI-generated images, AI-generated video clips, stickman or 2D animation, slideshow with text, screen recording, mixed. Default: AI-generated images.
2. **Art direction.** Free text or "you pick". Examples: cinematic realistic, flat illustration, anime, black-and-white sketch.
3. **Captions.** Options: burned-in, separate subtitle file, none. Default: burned-in.

## Group 4: Voice and audio

1. **Voiceover source.** Options: cloud text-to-speech, local text-to-speech, I record myself, no voiceover. Default: cloud text-to-speech.
2. **Voice choice.** Ask only if voiceover uses text-to-speech. Provider, voice name or style, speaking speed.
3. **Background music.** Options: none, generated, royalty-free library, my own files. Default: royalty-free library.
4. **Sound effects.** Options: yes, no. Default: no.

## Group 5: Tools and accounts

1. **Which providers do you have?** Multi-select: text-to-speech, image generation, video generation, stock footage, none yet. For each selected, ask the provider name and the environment variable that holds its key. Never ask for the key itself.
2. **Budget per video.** Free text or "no limit". Default: ask before any paid step.

## Group 6: Working style

1. **Approval level.** Options: approve every stage, approve only the story and final video, fully automatic after story. Default: approve every stage.
2. **Output folder.** Default: `~/.long-video/projects/<slug>/`.
3. **Anything else the workflow must respect?** Free text. Examples: brand colors, banned topics, intro and outro clips.

## After the groups

Show the full summary. Ask for changes. Then ask for the config name.
