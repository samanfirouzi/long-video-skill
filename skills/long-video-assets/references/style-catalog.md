# Style catalog

A pool of starting points. Pick and adapt. Do not list them all. Choose 10 that fit the story and the config, and tune each to the story's mood and palette.

If the config's `visuals.approach` is narrow (for example `animation-2d`), take all 10 from inside that approach and vary the look within it.

For round 2 and later, take styles not shown yet. If the pool runs out, invent new ones by mixing a medium, an era or culture, and a mood. Never repeat.

## Card format

```
<number>. <Style name>
Look: <one line: medium, line, color, lighting>
Fits because: <one line tied to this story>
```

## Choosing well

- Match the tone: a sad story does not fit a bright flat cartoon unless the contrast is deliberate.
- Match the era and place: period stories fit period art or archival looks.
- Match the audience: children, general, mature.
- Prefer styles an image generator holds steady across many images: strong shapes, a clear palette, simple lighting. Warn the user when a style is hard to keep consistent (for example, highly detailed photorealism with many faces).
- Give variety: across the 10, cover at least 4 different media or looks.
- Never name a living artist, studio or brand. Describe the look.
